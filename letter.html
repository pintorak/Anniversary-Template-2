A Letter
For You ♡

some things are easier to write than to say
FOR YOU, ALWAYS
08.10.25
My love,

I don't know if there will ever be enough words to explain everything I feel for you. But I know that meeting you became one of the most meaningful parts of my life.

We've had beautiful days, difficult days, moments when we understood each other without saying much, and moments when understanding each other wasn't easy at all.

But somehow, through all of them, you became someone I never wanted to be just a memory.

Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every little thing you did for me, and every memory we've created together.

I don't need our story to be perfect. I just want it to be honest, meaningful, and worth choosing again and again.

Wherever the next chapter takes us, I hope you always remember this — I'm grateful that it was you.

WITH ALL MY LOVE
yours ♡
